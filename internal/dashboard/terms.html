To use the EnMaaS AI Gateway, you must read and acknowledge Red Hat's policy on the Use of AI Technology. The policy describes Red Hat's requirements and guidelines for using AI and code-assistant tools.

The policy text is owned and maintained by Red Hat Legal at the link above. EnMaaS links to the authoritative policy instead of copying it here, so the version you acknowledge is always the current Red Hat policy.

What the acknowledgement records

When you check the acknowledgement box and submit it, EnMaaS records that your Red Hat identity acknowledged the policy for this EnMaaS terms version. The record contains the identity, policy version, timestamp, and the method used — it does not store a copy of the policy text.

EnMaaS service expectations

In addition to the Red Hat policy, use EnMaaS for Red Hat business purposes, keep API keys in an approved secret store, never share keys, and do not send secrets, regulated data, or customer data to a model or environment that is not approved for that data class. Some frontier models process prompts and responses through external model providers.

Questions about the policy or this acknowledgement: contact the EnMaaS team.
